About

Immerse yourself within the colorful ever-changing soul of Seattle, Pike Place Market. The oldest running farmer’s market in the country is rich with history and it’s brimming with delicious food and vibrant characters. Join us for an hour long cornucopia of everything that is and was the Market we have all come to love. Expect a belly full of samples, vendor interaction, history, and a great guide to lead you to the Market’s hidden secrets. Space is limited. Registration is required. Highlight
