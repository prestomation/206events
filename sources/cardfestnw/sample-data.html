What is Cardfest NW?

What is Cardfest NW?

Cardfest NW is a collectibles show series bringing together hundreds of vendors and thousands of collectors across the Pacific Northwest. Whether you’re hunting for a vintage single, looking to move a collection, or just want to see what’s out there - you’ll find it here.

Cardfest NW is a collectibles show series bringing together hundreds of vendors and thousands of collectors across the Pacific Northwest. Whether you’re hunting for a vintage single, looking to move a collection, or just want to see what’s out there - you’ll find it here.
