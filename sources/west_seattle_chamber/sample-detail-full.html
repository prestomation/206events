Description


Business After Hours heads to West Seattle Coworking, a locally owned shared workspace offering flexible offices, meeting rooms, and business grade internet for professionals who want a place to focus and connect.

WS Coworking was intentionally designed to support collaboration and real working relationships across the West Seattle business community.

In person networking still matters because trust is built faster face to face and visibility grows when people recognize you beyond a logo. This evening is designed for easy conversations, and new connections in a relaxed setting that could be the spot for your next meeting!
