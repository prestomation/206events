Description

Counseling West Seattle's Men's therapy group is open for enrollment!

Hosted by Matthew Kirshman, MA, MSW, LICSW, the group meets every Tuesday from 6:00–7:15 p.m. in person at Counseling West Seattle's offices on California Avenue. Topics are open-ended, focusing on issues involving communication, emotions, & identity, and how those topics are contextualized in male socialization.

Prospective participants will have an initial one-on-one session to ensure that this group is right for them.

Insurance information is available upon request.
