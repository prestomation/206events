About the Festival

The Unbound Symphony Summer Festival is a three-day gathering of approximately fifty professional musicians in the pacific northwest. Our goal is to celebrate and empower women and gender-marginalized musicians and use music as a way to express ourselves artistically and uplift the community.

Participants rehearse orchestral repertoire, take part in workshops, presentations or activities, and conclude with a public, family-friendly performance.

More information on our 2027 Summer Festival will be published and announced in the Spring.
