Enjoy live chamber music performed by musicians from Unbound Symphony at three lunchtime pop-up concerts outdoors in downtown Seattle, presented as part of the Met Tract Summer Sounds concert series.

Join us for an hour of live music in the heart of the city. These performances are FREE and open to the public. Come join us for lunch in the upper plaza, enjoy some music, and say hello!

📍 Location
1200 Fifth, Upper Plaza (outside the iconic IBM Building)
Address: 1200 5th Avenue, Seattle 98101

📅 Dates
July 22, July 29, and August 5

🕛 Time
12:00–1:00 PM

July 22 Jenn Shoval (viola), Sooyean Kim (violin), Erika Fiebig (cello), Bhavani Kotha (oboe) will present a chamber music program featuring Althea Talbot-Howard's Seville Cathedral, A Study in Memory – in Arch Form (2022).

July 29 Dr. Rachel Reyes (flute), Taylor DeCastro (violin), Jenn Shoval (viola), Erika Pierson (cello) will present a chamber music program featuring Emilie Mayer’s Quartet in A major (1856).

August 5 Jenny Ziefel (clarinet), Samantha Epp (Bassoon), Bhavani Kotha (oboe) will present a chamber music program featuring Jenni Brandon’s Sequia Trio (2009).
