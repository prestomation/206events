Daybreak Community & Land Tending

September 17, 11am-3pm @ Daybreak Star

We invite you to join us in tending the land at Daybreak Star Cultural Center. These gatherings are a return—to land, to care, and to being in right relationship with one another. Guided by Indigenous leadership at the United Indians of All Tribes Foundation, we come together to practice food sovereignty, cultural memory, and collective care in ways that are grounded, relational, and real. This season, we are tending the Forest Garden, a living space that supports elders, youth, and Indigenous-centered community programs while growing a future rooted in reciprocity, responsibility, and long-term care. All ages are welcome. You’re invited to come move your body, tend the land, and be in community—whether you stay for a couple of hours or the full day. Together, we’ll engage in hands-on care of the land through planting, weeding, harvesting, and seasonal projects, while also making space for connection, learning, and shared reflection. If you’re looking for a place to show up, build relationships, and grow something lasting, this is a space to gather, care for the land, and imagine what’s possible—together. All ages OK more

Community Printmaking with Eileen Jiminez at Seward Park

September 26, 11am-1pm @ Seward Park

Happy Latine Conservation Month! Join us for a morning of creating art on the land through printmaking with Eileen Jimenez! Participants will get to explore Connection to Land with pre-carved blocks and water coloring that centers land, place, and joy. Eileen Jiminez's Bio: Eileen's mother is Maria Cruz, her grandmother is Eloisa, and her great grandmother is Ysidora, matriarchs of the Ñätho (Otomi Peoples of Michoacan/Guanajuato, Mexico). As an IndigiQueer leader, community member, educator and as an artist, everything she does and creates is influenced by her many intersecting and lived experiences. Eileen uses printmaking and mixed-media techniques to develop her own ways of telling stories in the complex layers that they exist in, as well as to demonstrate the ways that we are connected to the Land and to each other. In her current body of work, she focuses on the embodiment of the divine that is manifested through our bodies and our hands specifically. In her work, you will also see her commitment to centering the stories of joy that flow through our communities. instagram: maese.art.by.eileen.jimenez website: www.eileenjimenez.com Food will be provided. ¡Feliz Mes de la Conservación Latine! ¡Acompáñanos a una mañana de creación artística en contacto con la tierra a través del grabado con Eileen Jimenez! Los participantes explorarán la conexión con la tierra utilizando bloques previamente tallados y acuarelas, en una actividad centrada en la tierra, el lugar y la alegría. Biografía de Eileen Jimenez: La madre de Eileen es Maria Cruz, su abuela es Eloisa y su bisabuela es Ysidora; todas ellas matriarcas del pueblo Ñätho (pueblo otomí de Michoacán y Guanajuato, México). Como líder, miembro de la comunidad, educadora y artista *IndigiQueer*, todo lo que hace y crea está influenciado por sus múltiples experiencias vitales y sus identidades entrelazadas. Eileen emplea técnicas de grabado y técnicas mixtas para desarrollar sus propias formas de narrar historias —respetando las complejas capas en las que estas existen— y para mostrar los vínculos que nos unen a la Tierra y entre nosotros. Su obra actual se centra en la encarnación de lo divino, manifestada específicamente a través de nuestros cuerpos y nuestras manos. En su trabajo también se refleja su compromiso de poner en primer plano las historias de alegría que fluyen por nuestras comunidades. Instagram: maese.art.by.eileen.jimenez Sitio web: www.eileenjimenez.com Se ofrecerá comida. more

Latine Conservation Month: ¡Vamos a Observar Aves!

September 26, 10am-12:30pm @ Seward Park

Celebrate Latine Conservation Month with a guided bird walk in the park! The walk will follow the outer loop of Seward Park, the Seward Park Loop Trail – a 2.4-mile paved path. Explore local wildlife, learn bird identification skills, and connect with others who share a passion for nature and conservation. This event welcomes birders of all experience levels and offers an opportunity to honor the rich contributions of Latine communities to environmental conservation. A Spanish-language interpreter will be present to support Spanish-speaking participants and help make the experience more accessible. Come enjoy fresh air, community, and the beauty of our local birds. 🐦🌿 ¡Celebra el Mes de la Conservación Latine con una caminata guiada de observación de aves en el parque! La caminata recorrerá el circuito exterior de Seward Park, el Sendero Seward Park Loop Trail, un camino pavimentado de 2.4 millas (3.9 km). Explora la vida silvestre local, aprende habilidades de identificación de aves y conéctate con otras personas que comparten la pasión por la naturaleza y la conservación. Este evento da la bienvenida a observadores de aves de todos los niveles de experiencia y ofrece la oportunidad de honrar las ricas contribuciones de las comunidades Latine a la conservación ambiental. Habrá un intérprete de español presente para apoyar a los participantes de habla hispana y ayudar a que la experiencia sea más accesible. Ven a disfrutar del aire fresco, la comunidad y la belleza de nuestras aves locales. 🐦🌿 more
