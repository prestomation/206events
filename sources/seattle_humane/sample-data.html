Show your purride with Seattle Humane!

Thank you everyone who joined us at the Seattle Pride Parade on June 28. We marched to celebrate kindness, inclusion and the power of community. It’s not too late to purchase our 2026 Pride merch! Every purchase helps save lives, complete families and keep pets with their people.

Get ready for Day of Giving!

Seattle Humane’s annual Day of Giving is upon us. Join us on September 15, 2026! Day of Giving is one of Seattle Humane’s most important fundraisers and is all about uniting our community to support our lifesaving work throughout the year.

Events at the Shelter

The following classes and events are all held at Seattle Humane, 13212 SE Eastgate Way, Bellevue, WA. 

Summer Camp

Each day at camp features animal interactions, games, guest speakers and creative crafts. Camps run Monday to Thursday, 8:30 a.m.-3:30 p.m.

Tuition is $695.

Registration is now open for Kids Day Camp and Teen Day Camp!

PAX West

September 4–7 | 10 a.m. – 6 p.m.
Washington State Convention Center, Arch and Summit Buildings

Take a break from the convention and recharge with kittens! Seattle Humane will be at PAX West with our popular Kitten Cuddle Booths and exclusive gaming merch, with every purchase helping support the animals in our care.

Friday, September 11 | 3 – 8 p.m.
Saturday, September 12 | 12 – 5 p.m.
South Lake Union Discovery Center Lawn

We’re excited to be part of Cider Summit Seattle! Visit Seattle Humane’s table to connect with fellow animal lovers, learn about our programs, and find out how you can help create a more compassionate community for pets and the people who love them.

Sea-Meow Con

November 7 & 8 | 10 a.m. – 5 p.m.
Seattle Center Exhibition Hall

Join Seattle Humane at SeaMeowCon in Kitten Alley for kitten cuddles, Seattle Humane merchandise, and resources to help pets and the people who love them. Stop by our booth to meet adorable kittens, shop for a cause, and learn about the services and support available in our community.

Learn more

Dog Training

A well-trained dog is a happy dog, and that makes for a happy owner! Seattle Humane offers a variety of dog and puppy training courses. Our effective, positive-reinforcement based training classes focus on good manners in the real world, socialization, bonding and giving you the tools to prevent or address behavior challenges. For more information and a list of upcoming courses, please visit the dog training center.
