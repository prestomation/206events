We can’t wait to share more inspiring and memorable concert experiences with you! We’re putting the finishing touches on our season subscriptions and single tickets and will be sharing our with ticket sales pages with you very soon. We’ll be returning to Tacoma, Lynnwood and Seattle for live concerts, masterclasses and Cantata Trail sessions, as well as sharing our video content on social media and YouTube for a growing audience at home and around the world. Below are the dates and brief descriptions of our concerts for the 2026/2027 season – mark your calendars and watch for our ticket announcement!

Day of Historical Keyboards

October 3, 2026 | 10 am – 4 pm

Back by popular demand, the Day of Historical Keyboards returns for another inspiring celebration of music, craftsmanship, and discovery! Throughout the day, enjoy lively 30-minute recitals and engaging Q&A sessions showcasing the distinctive sounds and fascinating histories of the lautenwerck, clavichord, harpsichord, fortepiano, organ, and more.

Meet knowledgeable performers, hear these remarkable instruments in action, and gain new insight into how keyboard music has evolved across the centuries. Whether you are a longtime enthusiast, a curious newcomer, a student, or a musician, there will be something to discover. Come listen, learn, ask questions, and even try the instruments yourself!

Bach’s B Minor Mass

October 23, 24, 25 2026

Experience the absolute pinnacle of the baroque in the Pacific Northwest. As a proud marquee highlight of the Early Music America Summit in Seattle, the Seattle Bach Festival invites you to a transcendent performance of J.S. Bach’s Mass in B Minor—widely considered one of the greatest human achievements in musical history.

A German Advent

December 11, 12, 13 2026

A festive celebration of the Advent season, this program features 17th-century delights from the generations before Bach. We are thrilled to feature the Dark Horse consort of sackbuts (early trombones), violins, continuo, and a quintet of voices. A glorious and festive way to celebrate the magic of the season!

Bach’s Christmas Oratorio + Brandenburg 1

January 8, 9, 10 2027

Begin the new year with the warmth and brilliance of Bach as the Seattle Bach Festival continues its presentation of J.S. Bach’s magnificent Christmas Oratorio (Weihnachts-Oratorium). Following last season’s performance of Parts I–III, this year’s program completes the Nativity story with Parts 4, 5, and 6 paired with the dazzling orchestral colors of Bach’s Brandenburg Concerto No. 1.

Bach’s St. Matthew Passion

March 19, 20, 21 2027

Bach’s St. Matthew Passion unfolds as gripping musical theater, moving between urgent storytelling, intimate reflection, and the tremendous power of two choirs and two orchestras. The work draws listeners into the drama through vivid choruses, expressive arias, and moments of profound stillness, revealing both the scale of the Passion story and the deeply personal emotions within it. Join the Seattle Bach Festival for an unforgettable encounter with a masterpiece of extraordinary beauty, complexity, and humanity.
