U District Events

The neighborhood is proud to host major public events that celebrate the U District neighborhood; its history, character, and the people behind the businesses that make this neighborhood a welcoming destination for all!

Seattle Boba Fest

August 1, 2026

The U District is Seattle’s destination for Boba, or Bubble Tea! This April, Seattle Boba Fest will return to the U District with over 20 bubble tea and other restaurants who will be ready to delight your taste buds or simply teach you ‘how to Boba.’

Learn More

U District Chow Down & Street Party

October 3, 2026

The U District celebrates the end of summer with a neighborhood-wide food walk and street party celebration. Find bites at nearly 60 participating businesses and dance along to live music and entertainment with an outdoor stage on the Ave!

Learn More

U District Cherry Blossom Festival

Spring 2027

90 local U Districts will be serving up unique cherry-inspired bites, drinks and specials to celebrate the Cherry Blossom Festival. This promotion coincides with the University of Washington’s world-renowned cherry blossom bloom in late March on the UW Quad, a tradition that brings people from all over the region.

Learn More

U District Street Fair

Saturday May 15 & Sunday, May 16, 2027

The U District Street Fair is the longest running festival of its kind in the nation. The third weekend in May each year marks the return of this annual tradition and kicks off the festival season in Seattle by closing down the Ave to bring makers, artists, food vendors, bands and to highlight the unique small businesses of the U District.

Learn More

Other Events & Performance Venues

Neptune Theatre

stgpresents.org/neptune/events

Neptune Theatre is a historic venue in Seattle’s University District, offers a captivating setting for events with its grand architecture and rich theatrical heritage, making it an ideal choice for performances, conferences, and cultural gatherings.

Jet City Improv

jetcityimprov.org/

Jet City Improv, located in the heart of Seattle, is the go-to destination for hilarious and interactive entertainment, providing a dynamic space for improv shows and team-building events that promise laughter and a unique, engaging experience.

Meany Hall

meanycenter.org

Meany Center for the Performing Arts at the University of Washington stands as a distinguished event space, hosting a diverse range of performances from classical concerts to avant-garde productions, offering an elegant and acoustically refined setting for cultural events and artistic performances.

Barnes & Noble - U District

stores.barnesandnoble.com/store/3563

The Barnes&Noble book store located at the famed University Book Store serves as a versatile venue for literary events, book signings, and author talks.

Burke Museum

burkemuseum.org

With its fascinating exhibits and modern event spaces, The Burke Museum is an ideal choice for those seeking a distinctive venue that combines education and entertainment, making it perfect for receptions, fundraisers, and events that inspire curiosity.

U District Art Walk

udistrictartwalk.org

On the third Friday of every month, make your way through the U District for an evening art walk to view work by local artists around the neighborhood

Henry Art Gallery

henryart.org

The Henry Art Gallery offers a contemporary and visually stimulating setting for events, making it a premier choice for art exhibitions, gallery openings, and avant-garde performances in Seattle’s vibrant arts community.

UHeights Center

uheightscenter.org/upcoming-events

UHeights Center, nestled in the U District, is a community hub that provides versatile spaces for events, performances, and workshops, fostering creativity and connection within a welcoming and inclusive environment.

Laughs Comedy Club

laughscomedyclub.com/

At the heart of Seattle’s comedy scene, Laughs Comedy Club is the ultimate destination for established and up-and-coming comedians. Offering a lively venue for stand-up comedy shows and events that guarantee a night of hilarity and good times.

UW Drama

drama.washington.edu

UW Drama, situated within the University of Washington, is a dynamic and versatile venue that cultivates creativity and innovation in the performing arts. With state-of-the-art facilities and a commitment to theatrical excellence, UW Drama is the perfect space for immersive performances, experimental plays, and engaging events that push the boundaries of the theatrical experience.

Nightlife Fun

The U District offers exciting nightlife across local cafes and bars! Check out these regular events:

Bulldog News – offering monthly open mic nights and poetry readings.

The College Inn Pub – features Trivia Tuesdays and other events throughout the year

Big Time Brewery – plays host to Chess Club every Tuesday night and Trivia on Wednesdays

Seattle Records & Cassettes and Innervisions Posters – host open mic nights, eighties nights, record shows, and more on their calendar across their two locations.
