Public-speaking practice clubs. Seattle has a dozen-plus independent
Toastmasters clubs (Northeast Seattle, West Seattle, Seattle Sunrise, Toast
of the Town, Seattle General, Seattle Speaking Project, etc.), each a
separate small nonprofit with its own site and weekly/biweekly meeting
schedule; no citywide aggregator or shared calendar platform.

Investigated 2026-09-18: each club's meeting is a generic recurring
"club meeting" (same format every week, no distinct per-meeting content) —
same shape as internal-meeting sources already deprioritized elsewhere
(League of Women Voters chapters, quilting-guild business meetings). No
single club has enough distinct public-facing content to justify its own
source, and building one `recurring/` entry per club (12+) for
indistinguishable weekly meetings isn't a good use of the calendar. Not
implemented.
