Regional theater company producing musicals and plays — but both of its
venues are in Issaquah and Everett, not Seattle proper. Per the
source-discovery quality gate ("venues entirely outside Seattle city
limits are not appropriate"), and consistent with the `norwescon.md`
precedent, this is not viable: it has no Seattle-proper location at all,
unlike sources with primarily-Seattle events plus a few nearby.

Real, live site. 2026-2027 mainstage season lists 5 productions (Ragtime,
Come From Away, And Then There Were None, Dear Evan Hansen, Love Is Dead)
plus youth-education shows (e.g. Anastasia). Ticket purchase links route to
`purchase.villagetheatre.org`, a dedicated subdomain whose backing vendor
(AudienceView/Vendini/Tessitura/etc.) wasn't confirmed from the fetched
content. Volume is modest (5-7 shows/season) since it's a season-based
theater rather than a frequent-event venue.
