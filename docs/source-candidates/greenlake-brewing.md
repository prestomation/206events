New neighborhood brewery from Cody Cluff, who also owns Gasworks Brewing
(already tracked — `docs/source-candidates/gasworks-brewing.md`,
`status: notviable`) and Magnuson Brewery (not previously tracked; not
investigated in this run). Opened April 7, 2026 in the former Wooden
City Tavern space at 7110 E Green Lake Drive N, Seattle. Full kitchen,
weekend brunch, beer/cider/wine/cocktails.

No dedicated website or events page could be found via search — coverage
so far is limited to local news articles about the opening (Hoodline, KIRO
7, Washington Beer Blog) and general neighborhood-venue listings (Do206,
Eventbrite "Green Lake" search), none of which are the brewery's own feed.
Revisit in a future cycle once the brewery has been open longer and likely
stands up its own site/social presence with a findable events feed.
