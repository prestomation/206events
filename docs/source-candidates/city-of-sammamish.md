City of Sammamish (King County) publishes events at
`sammamishwa.gov/news/events/`, but this is a different platform than the
other King County CivicPlus city sites already covered (Issaquah, North
Bend, Enumclaw, Snoqualmie) — no `iCalendar.aspx`/`.ics`/RSS link found on
the page. The site links out to `sammamishwa.civicweb.net`, which is
CivicWeb's meeting-agenda portal, not an events feed. No public
machine-readable calendar found. Not viable without a custom HTML
scraper of the events page itself (no structured JSON/ICS observed) —
revisit if a feed shows up later.
