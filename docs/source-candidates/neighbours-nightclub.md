Seattle's oldest continuously-operating LGBTQ+ nightclub, 1509 Broadway,
Capitol Hill. Advertises a themed weekly schedule (talent night, "Pulse
Neighbours", "Rock Lobster Party", "The Ultimate Fridays", "Powermix
Saturdays", "Noche Latina").

Investigated 2026-09-16:
- Site is a WordPress theme, but all linked post permalinks on the
  homepage date to **2012-2013** — the content (including the themed-night
  copy) appears to be a long-unmaintained static snapshot rather than a
  live schedule.
- No specific start times found anywhere on the homepage or the dedicated
  `/hours/` page — only vague day names and a general "21+ Tuesday-Sunday,
  18+ Wednesday" age-policy blurb. Third-party listings (Yelp) give
  conflicting operating hours from what the club's own site implies (e.g.
  "Monday/Tuesday closed" vs. a Tuesday talent-night promo on-site).
- No ICS, no Eventbrite/Ticketmaster/DICE presence found.
- Given the stale content and lack of a single reliable start time per
  night, this doesn't meet the bar for a `sources/recurring/` entry the
  way `southgate-roller-rink` or `rough-and-tumble-pub` did (both of which
  had current, specific, cross-confirmed day+time schedules).
- Likely partial coverage already exists via the newly-added `do206`
  aggregator (broad concerts/comedy/culture listings across Seattle
  venues, added 2026-09-15).

**Verdict**: Not viable today — re-check if the site is ever refreshed
