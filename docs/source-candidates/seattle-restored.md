City of Seattle Office of Economic Development program that places pop-up
shops and art installations in vacant downtown storefronts. Investigated
2026-08-08: no dated events calendar, no ICS feed, no API. The site's
"News & Events" section is blog-style posts about which storefronts are
active, and the program leans on Instagram (`@seattlerestored`) for
grand-opening announcements and hours rather than a structured, dated event
listing. Nothing here maps to a `RipperCalendarEvent` (no start times/dates
per activation). Re-evaluate if the org ever publishes a real calendar.
