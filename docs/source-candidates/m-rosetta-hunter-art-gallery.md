Hill). Found via "Seattle art gallery events calendar opening
reception" search.

**Investigated 2026-08-26:** Only ~3 exhibitions per year, and the
"Upcoming" page lists them with month-level date ranges only (e.g.
"August - October 2026", "November 2026") — no specific opening dates
or times. Gallery hours themselves are irregular ("often, but not
always" open 2nd Thursdays for Capitol Hill Art Walk, closed entirely
June-September). No ICS/JSON feed; page is plain server-rendered
Drupal HTML with no structured event data. Not enough dated
information to produce real calendar events. Revisit if the gallery
starts publishing specific opening dates/times.
