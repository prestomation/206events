Nextdoor neighborhood social network events calendar for Seattle area community gatherings.

**Findings (2026-08-14):** Not a viable source. This is user-generated content on a walled
social platform (yard sales, informal meetups posted by individual neighbors) — no public
API/ICS feed, full interaction/detail requires a Nextdoor account, and scraping a major
social platform's UGC listings is both fragile (no stable structure/IDs) and likely against
its terms of service. Not the kind of curated organizational source this project wants.
