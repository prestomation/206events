Requested via GitHub issue #1256 ("Add the Seattle sunset market on
September 10") — the in-app feedback form. Identified as the "Met Tract
Sunset Market", a monthly market (2nd Thursday) at Metropolitan Tract,
1200 5th Ave, Seattle, WA 98101 (the block bounded by 4th/5th/University/
Union downtown). Confirmed a Sept 10, 2026 3-7pm occurrence matches
exactly.

The venue runs a WordPress "The Events Calendar" (Tribe Events) plugin
with a public ICS feed covering all of its own programming, not just the
Sunset Market — live-fetched and confirmed 6 upcoming events including
"Met Tract Sunset Market" (2026-09-10), "Met Tract Summer Sounds",
"Different Strokes Art Market", and recurring "The Reference Center" /
"sunday art cafe" programs:

```
https://metropolitantract.com/?post_type=tribe_events&ical=1&eventDisplay=list
```

This is distinct from `sources/unbound_symphony` (Unbound Symphony's own
concert pages, one of which happens to be a pop-up series held on the
Met Tract plaza) — this source is the venue's own general events
calendar, a different org and a broader feed.

Not a religious source; secular commercial/civic property (owned by the
University of Washington, home to office towers, retail, and a public
plaza) that runs its own public programming calendar.
