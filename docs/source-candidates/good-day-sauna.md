Mobile wood-fired sauna / cold-plunge outfit found via a "Seattle sauna"
search, regularly parked at Lincoln Park (West Seattle) plus private
pop-ups. Squarespace-hosted, but `?format=json` on the homepage returns an
empty `mainContent` block, and the rendered page is entirely FAQ/booking
copy ("Can I cancel or reschedule your session up to 24 hours in
advance?", 18+ age limit, cancellation policy) — this is a reservation
business selling timed sauna sessions, not a publicly-listed events
calendar. No dated, open-to-the-public event schedule to scrape.

**Verdict**: Not viable — booking/reservation service, no public events
calendar. Same shape as `blade-and-timber.md`.
