Statewide (not Seattle-specific) directory of recurring blues jam / open
mic nights, organized by day-of-week rather than dated events. No
ICS/RSS/API — the page states the listing "is then downloaded for
printing in our WBS Bluesletter on the 10th of every month" from a
Google Sheets submission form, i.e. a static weekly directory, not a
calendar of dated events. Most Seattle venues named on the page (Nectar
Lounge, Central Saloon) are already covered by their own sources. Not a
scrapable event feed.
