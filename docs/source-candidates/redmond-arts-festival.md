15th Annual outdoor arts festival at Redmond Town Center in Redmond, WA.
Three days (July 31 - August 2, 2026), free admission. Organized by VALA
Eastside in partnership with Redmond Town Center.

Features 60+ artists, live music on the EvergreenHealth Music Stage, family
activities. Full schedule with set times available on the website.

This is a recurring annual festival (15th year). Similar to other annual
festival sources like bite-of-seattle, bumbershoot, bellevue-arts-fair.
