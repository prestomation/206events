Annual community festival in Magnolia (3 days, typically late July/early August).
Held at Magnolia Playfield (W Smith St & 33rd Ave W) with a parade route through
the neighborhood. Presented by the Magnolia Chamber of Commerce (discovermagnolia.org).

The schedule page at magnoliasummerfest.org/schedule was empty at time of check
("This block has no content yet") — likely populated closer to the event.

May already be covered by the `discover-magnolia` external ICS feed
(discovermagnolia.org events), but that feed is a general Magnolia community
calendar. Summerfest deserves its own entry as a major annual festival, similar
to bite-of-seattle, beacon-hill-festival, etc.
