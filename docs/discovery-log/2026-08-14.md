## Source discovery: uncouchme-batch vetting + community centers, art walks, new venues, nonprofits, car meets, sci-fi conventions

The previous PR (#1197) bulk-imported 134 placeholder candidate files
(`platform: Unknown`, `status: candidate`) from a comparison against
uncouchme.com, with no individual vetting. Today's run focused on
draining that backlog — quality-gating each one against the standard
checklist (Seattle-focus, public events page/feed, platform match,
not already covered, event volume, not a religious org) — plus a
smaller round of fresh searches in verticals not covered recently.

### Backlog vetting (134 files → all resolved)

- **56 → `candidate`** (real platform confirmed, still viable, not yet
  covered)
- **54 → `notviable`** (already covered by an existing source, not
  Seattle-focused, religious org, static/undated content, or dead
  link)
- **24 → `investigating`** (SiteGround `sgcaptcha` / Cloudflare JS
  challenges blocked every fetch attempt, or the dated schedule page
  couldn't be located — needs a different approach, not abandoned)

Notable duplicate catches (already covered elsewhere, flipped to
`notviable`): Taproot Theatre, Sunset Tavern, STG Presents, Seattle
Storm (`climate_pledge_arena`), The Crocodile (Live Nation),
Lumen Field Events, Mariners Promotions, Vera Project, Tractor Tavern,
Vice Seattle (`external/mohai.yaml`), Darrell's Tavern
(`seattle_showlists`), Emerald City Comedy, Fleet Feet Fun Runs, and
Pike Pine Social (fully composed of four already-implemented sources).

  (`folkdance.page/index.ics?date=all&city=Seattle`) returning 333
  real `VEVENT`s. Highest-confidence pick in the batch.

  JSON API** (`data.kingcounty.gov/resource/grxi-zqg2.json`) with
  clean structured events including lat/lng.

  ~350 events/yr; scope spans WA/OR/BC so would need Puget Sound

- 💡 **Not Creepy Gathering** — confirmed Humanitix ticketing with
  per-event Google Calendar/ICS export links.
- 💡 **Saving Great Animals** — WordPress + The Events Calendar (Tribe
  Events) plugin, visible iCal export links.
- 💡 **Savoy Swing** — Squarespace, 20+ dated events, per-event
  `?format=ical` links confirmed live.
- 💡 **West Seattle Indivisible** — Squarespace, 50+ dated events
  (Jul–Sep 2026).
- 💡 **NW Dance** (nw-dance.net) — WordPress "Modern Events Calendar"
  plugin, real Aug–Nov 2026 events.
- 💡 **Sound Theatre Company** — uses OvationTix, the same platform as
  the existing `taproot` ripper; likely near config-only via the
  built-in `ovationtix` type once a `clientId` is found.

  listing; a follow-up should locate the organizer ID.

Full per-candidate notes (platform confirmation, event counts, caveats)
are in each file under `docs/source-candidates/`.

### Fresh searches (new verticals: community centers, art walks, new
venues, nonprofits/fundraisers, car meets, sci-fi conventions)

Most leads resolved to sources already covered — Seattle Center Festál
and all of the city's neighborhood art walks (Ballard, Belltown,
Georgetown, Pioneer Square, University District, West Seattle,
Capitol Hill, Fremont, etc.) already have dedicated recurring/ripper
sources; EverOut, AllEvents.in, Eventbrite category pages, Norwescon,
and Sakura-Con were all already tracked candidates.

Two genuinely new leads:

- ✅ **Candidate added: Rigs & Coffee at Tiki Motors** — monthly car
  meet (3rd Saturday, 10am–12pm) at 5201 1st Ave S, SoDo. No ICS/API,
  but the recurring pattern is confirmed via third-party car-meet
  directories; shaped like the already-implemented
  `sources/recurring/lake-washington-cars-and-coffee.yaml`.
- 💡 **Candidate added: GeekGirlCon** — annual convention at Seattle
  Convention Center (Oct 24–25, 2026), WordPress site with no
  discoverable ICS/API. Single annual 2-day event, so — like
  Sakura-Con — kept low-priority until a machine-readable source
  surfaces.

The Shop Clubs (SoDo car club) was investigated but skipped — its
event calendar is client-rendered and membership requirements were
unclear on a shallow fetch; not logged as a candidate per the "no
structured calendar found" convention.

### Dead sources

Ran `dead-sources.py` against production `build-errors.json` at the
start of the run. All 7 zero-event calendars and 1 external failure
it flagged (Frye Art Museum, Black Lodge, Good Society Beer, On the
Hiyu, Seattle Children's Museum, SeattleDances, Urban League Seattle,
West Seattle Blog) are already-`added`/`proxy` sources with existing
candidate files — a single build snapshot doesn't meet the 30-day
dead-source bar, so none were flagged `dead` this cycle.

No source was implemented this cycle — today's run was scoped to
draining the candidate-vetting backlog and discovering new leads, not
implementation.
